FORMAT AUDITS
- Public Mir screencast audit exercised requested format IDs 1-7 at 1920x1080 and checked returned format, dimensions, stride, and buffer metadata.
- Venus audit enumerated the real OUTPUT queue and attempted TRY_FMT and S_FMT for every enumerated and relevant RGB candidate.

DIRECT RGB REJECTION
- Live Mir -> Venus RGB4 produced green/corrupt output.
- Four deterministic RGB4 trials supplied exact ABGR, XBGR, ARGB, and XRGB byte layouts with asymmetric colors/geometry.
- All four decoded images were incorrect and had the same SHA-256 prefix c511da; changing R/B and A/X layout did not fix it.
- Therefore direct RGB is rejected even though Venus accepts S_FMT and STREAMON.
